Behind the Shelter Doors

Where Australia’s animals go when they need a second chance.

Australia’s shelter system is not a single, uniform network. This project follows where pressure appears, when demand changes, and how outcomes differ for dogs and cats.

03 · Where care is physically located

RSPCA facilities cluster around Australia’s populated coast

Physical shelter and adoption facilities are not evenly distributed across the continent. This verified set of RSPCA-operated sites is concentrated around major coastal population centres, with much larger gaps across inland Australia.

Sources: official RSPCA state and territory shelter/location pages and RSPCA Adopt a Pet. Points represent verified RSPCA-operated shelters, animal care centres or adoption centres included in this working dataset; partner stores, council pounds and non-RSPCA shelters are not shown. Coordinates are geocoded from published addresses and are intended for national-scale location context.
