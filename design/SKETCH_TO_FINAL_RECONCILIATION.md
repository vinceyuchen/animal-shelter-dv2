## Explicitly provisional or incorrect notes in the original drawing

The sketch is a record of an *early plan*, not an authoritative statistical conclusion:

1. **Date and attribution:** the footer reads **“Date: 10 Apr 2025”** and **“Author: Your Name”**, inconsistent with the 2026 project and its final byline. These are visible in the original photographed sheet, are not silently corrected here, and should **not** be offered as evidence of the actual time or authorship of the DV2 sketch.
2. **Cat rehoming claim:** the footer suggests cats have *lower rehoming*. The final 2024–25 RSPCA species-outcome records show **64.1% of cat outcomes were Rehomed versus 43.1% of dog outcomes**. The finished website correctly uses those values.
3. **Annual-change rank:** the sketch's Chart 9 callout writes **SA “-9.7%”** in the 2024–25 top position. The final data say **South Australia +9.7%**, rank 1, and **Victoria -18.4%**, rank 6, for combined dog/cat year-on-year change. The sketch's negative sign for SA was a draft error.
4. **Tasmanian cat share:** the sketch prints a preliminary **89.1%** starting value. The final chart uses the sourced **89.9% (2020–21) to 64.9% (2024–25)**, a change of roughly **-25.0 percentage points**.
5. **Causal language:** the sketch includes “Why does euthanasia happen?” and suggests fostering/programs *can improve outcomes*. The available descriptive charts cannot establish causal effects. The final butterfly title asks **which reasons were recorded**, and the website avoids asserting that the observed data prove a particular intervention caused better outcomes.

These planning-stage corrections do **not** require falsifying or redrawing the original sketch. The final website, actual chart data and source notes govern the reported numerical findings.
